# Format — Music video

Beat-synced, dialogue-free, and the edit is the star.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Aspect** | 16:9 or 9:16 (9:16 for Reels/Shorts cuts) |
| **Length** | Full song — 2–4 min typical |
| **Shots** | ~1 shot per 2–4s of runtime → 40–90 shots |
| **Structure** | song sections, not story beats |
| **Shot grammar** | matching aspect file |

## Structure = the song

Map the song first. Every section gets a visual idea and a cutting rate:

| Section | Visual job | Cut rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Intro** | Establish the world. One strong image. | Slow, 4–6s |
| **Verse** | Narrative thread begins | 2–4s |
| **Pre-chorus** | Tighten — fewer locations, closer frames | 1.5–3s |
| **Chorus** | Biggest images, most movement | 1–2s |
| **Verse 2** | Narrative escalates, new location | 2–4s |
| **Bridge** | Break the pattern — different light, different lens | varies |
| **Final chorus** | Repeat the chorus visually, bigger | 1–2s |
| **Outro** | One image, held. Let it breathe. | 4–8s |

Write this as `plan.md` **before** any shot list. A music video planned shot-by-shot without the section map always feels random.

## Two threads

The reliable structure is **performance + narrative**, intercut:

- **Performance** — the artist singing/playing. Energy, lip sync, camera movement.
- **Narrative** — a small story, often symbolic, that resolves at the bridge.

Rule: **the narrative advances during verses, the performance owns the choruses.**

## Lip sync — the honest problem

AI lip sync is unreliable, especially beyond a few seconds and in any language.

Options:

1. **Avoid tight lip sync.** Shoot performance from behind, in silhouette, in wide shots, or with the mic obscuring the mouth.
2. **Match on energy, not mouth shape.** Cut on the beat, not on the syllable.
3. **Accept looseness.** Many successful AI music videos never show a clear mouth.
4. **Use your own footage** for the lip-sync close-ups.

If the user wants a straight-to-camera singing close-up, say early that this is the highest-risk shot in the project.

## Rhythm rules

- **Cut on the beat**, especially on the kick or snare.
- **Change shot size on the cut**, not just the image — wide → close → wide.
- **Hold longer than feels right** in the bridge; contrast is what makes the chorus hit.
- **One recurring image** that returns at each chorus — that's what makes it feel authored.

## Colour and light

- Chorus: brighter, more saturated, more movement.
- Verse: cooler, stiller, longer lenses.
- Bridge: invert it — if the video is warm, go monochrome.

## Deliverable deltas

- `plan.md` → section map with visual idea and cut rate per section
- `script.md` → not dialogue; instead **beat markers** (timestamps of musical hits)
- `caption.md` → track credit, art direction note, cover frame

## Failure modes

1. Shot list written without a section map
2. Same energy for 3 minutes
3. Narrative thread that never resolves
4. Tight lip sync attempted and failing
5. No recurring image — feels like a stock footage montage
