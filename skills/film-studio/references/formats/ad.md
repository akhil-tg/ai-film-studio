# Format — Ad / commercial

No character arc. The product is the protagonist, and the clock is 15–30 seconds.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Aspect** | 9:16 (social) or 16:9 (YouTube, TV-style) |
| **Length** | 15s / 30s / 60s |
| **Shots** | 4–9 at 15s · 8–15 at 30s |
| **Structure** | hook → problem → product → proof → CTA |
| **Shot grammar** | vertical or horizontal file, matching aspect |

## Structure — 30s

| Time | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Hook | A tension the viewer recognises |
| 2–8s | Problem | Show it, don't describe it |
| 8–15s | Product | First clear look. Label legible. |
| 15–24s | Proof | The benefit, demonstrated |
| 24–30s | CTA | One action, one line |

**15s version:** hook+problem (0–4) · product (4–9) · proof (9–13) · CTA (13–15).
**60s version:** add a second proof beat or a testimonial before the CTA.

## The four elements, replaced

Ads don't use character/want/obstacle/change. They use:

| Element | Question |
|---|---|
| **Tension** | What does the viewer already feel? |
| **Product role** | What does the product do about it? |
| **Proof** | What makes it believable? |
| **Action** | What exactly should they do? |

## Product handling

This is where AI ads fail. The product must be **consistent and legible**:

- Generate or photograph **one hero image of the product** first. It's a mandatory reference.
- Paste that reference into every shot containing the product.
- Keep the label readable — if it's blurry, the ad doesn't work.
- Avoid hands holding and turning the product: fine motor work degrades labels.
- Never let the model "improve" the product design.

## Copy

- **One message.** Two messages is two ads.
- **On-screen text carries the claim**, because many watch muted.
- Max 6 words per card.
- The CTA is a **verb + object**: "Order now", "Try free for 30 days".

## Compliance

- No claim you can't substantiate. "Clinically proven" requires a study.
- No before/after that misrepresents the result.
- Don't imitate a competitor's distinctive look or a celebrity's likeness.
- Disclose AI-generated imagery where the platform or law requires it.

## Deliverable deltas

- `story.md` → tension, product role, proof, action, mandatory product reference
- `story.md` → brand colours, logo placement, product angles
- `caption.md` → primary text, headline, CTA, legal line

## Failure modes

1. Product illegible or morphing between shots
2. Hook that describes rather than shows the problem
3. Feature list instead of one benefit
4. No CTA, or three CTAs
5. Looking like a film that happens to contain a product — it's an ad, lead with the product
